# Basic Flowchart of a DC Generator :  

START  
  │  
  ▼  
Atoms contain electrons  
  │  
  ▼  
Electrons have charge and spin  
  │  
  ▼  
Moving charges create tiny magnetic fields  
  │  
  ▼  
Each electron acts like a tiny magnet  
  │  
  ▼  
Magnetic moments of atoms are formed  
  │  
  ▼  
Are the magnetic moments aligned?  
      │  
      ├── NO  
      │      ▼  
      │  Magnetic effects cancel out  
      │      ▼  
      │  Material is not a magnet  
      │
      └── YES  
             ▼  
      Magnetic moments add together  
             ▼  
      Strong magnetic field is produced  
             ▼  
      Material becomes a magnet  
             ▼  
      North Pole (N) and South Pole (S) appear  
             ▼  
      Magnetic field exists between poles  
             ▼  
      Current flows through a conductor  
             ▼  
      Conductor is placed in the magnetic field  
             ▼  
      Magnetic field interacts with current  
             ▼  
      Force acts on the conductor  
             ▼  
      (Fleming's Left-Hand Rule)  
             ▼  
      Conductor moves  
             ▼  
      Many conductors experience force  
             ▼  
      Torque is produced  
             ▼  
      Armature rotates  
             ▼  
      DC MOTOR WORKS  
             ▼  
            END
